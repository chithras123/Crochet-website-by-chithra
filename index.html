About me

Handmade with love 🧶

Hi! I'm the hands and heart behind Tamaraa. What started as a little hobby with a hook and a ball of yarn has grown into a small business I truly adore.

Every scrunchie, bow and headband is crocheted by hand in small batches, using soft, good-quality yarn. No two pieces are exactly alike — that's the charm of handmade.

Want something in a special colour or size? I love custom orders — just send me a message!
